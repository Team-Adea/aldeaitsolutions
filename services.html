Services

Three kinds of work, one small team.

We're deliberately narrow. Everything below is work we've done before and can scope with confidence. Anything outside it, we'll say so.

01

Automation and integration

Removing repeated manual work from your week. We start with whatever costs the most hours.

Google Apps Script

Scripts and add-ons inside Sheets, Gmail, Docs, Drive and Forms. Scheduled reports, generated documents, validated data entry, approval flows.

Zapier automations

Connecting the tools you already pay for so nothing gets retyped. Built with error handling and a log, not a fragile one-off zap.

Spreadsheet and form systems

Turning a shared spreadsheet into something that behaves: validated entry via Forms, calculated fields, permissions, and a dashboard the owner can read without asking anyone.

02

Google Workspace and domains

Getting your organisation onto professional infrastructure and keeping it in your own hands.

Workspace migration

From personal Gmail, Microsoft 365 or a legacy host. Mail, files and calendars moved, users trained, a rollback plan in case.

Domain management

Registration and transfers, DNS records, SPF, DKIM and DMARC so your mail lands. All of it documented under accounts you own.

Workspace administration

Groups, shared drives, permissions, security settings and offboarding. Sensible defaults instead of everyone-can-see-everything.

03

Websites, portfolios and testing

A credible web presence, whether you are a company or one person, and testing that catches problems before your customers do.

Website design and build

Small business sites: fast, readable on a phone, editable by you. Domain, email and analytics set up alongside it.

Portfolio and one-page sites

For freelancers, consultants and solo professionals. One page that shows the work, states the rates and makes it easy to get in touch. Live in a week, on your own domain.

Software testing

A dedicated tester on your product: written test cases, structured manual passes and bug reports your developers can reproduce. On what you built or what we built.

Game testing

Functional and regression passes on builds for indie studios: reproducible repro steps, video capture where it helps, and a bug list triaged by severity rather than dumped in a spreadsheet.

Ongoing care

Updates, backups, uptime checks and small content changes on a monthly retainer if you would rather not think about it.

What we're honest about

We're still building depth in some newer automation platforms. If your project needs a tool we haven't shipped with yet, we'll tell you up front rather than learn on your budget.

Not on this list?

Ask anyway. If it's adjacent to what we do, we'll take a look. If it isn't, we'll try to point you to someone who does it well.
