Pricing

You'll know the number before we start.

We don't publish rate cards, because a two-day script and a 60-seat migration aren't the same job. What we do commit to is a written scope and a fixed price before any work begins. Four ways to engage us:

Most common
Fixed-scope project

One defined outcome: an automation, a migration, a website. Priced as a single number after the free consultation.

  • Written scope before invoicing
  • Half up front, half on handover
  • 30 days of free defect fixes
Quick turnaround
Small project

One clear deliverable with a deadline we agree on together — a fix, an audit, a small build. Priced as a single number, not billed by the hour.

  • One agreed deadline, not open-ended
  • Fixed price, no surprise hours
  • Good for quick fixes, audits, one-off builds
Ongoing
Monthly retainer

A set number of hours each month for changes, admin, monitoring and the small things that keep piling up.

  • Priority on support requests
  • Rolls into whatever needs doing
  • 30 days notice to stop, no lock-in
Best value
Annual plan

Build something with us once, then let us look after it for the year — updates, small changes and priority support, locked into one price.

  • One project + a year of care, bundled
  • Works out cheaper than paying monthly
  • Same priority support as retainer clients

How a quote comes together

01
